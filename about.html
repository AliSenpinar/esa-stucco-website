Our Story

About ESA Stucco LTD

The owners of ESA Stucco LTD have over 20 years of construction experience and have been delivering professional stucco and exterior finishing services since 2012. Our work is built on honest execution, reliable timelines, and results that stand up over time.

Built on Experience

Focused on Results

ESA Stucco LTD has built a strong reputation across the GTA and beyond by working with homeowners, custom builders, designers, architects, and commercial project teams that expect quality work and dependable communication.

From small repairs to full exterior finishing scopes, our approach stays the same: proper preparation, quality materials, fair pricing, and a clean final result. We don’t cut corners, and we treat every project with the same level of care.

Our Values
  • Quality Finish: clean lines, sharp details, and finished work that looks good up close.
  • Reliable Communication: clear expectations, straightforward pricing, and realistic timelines.
  • Professional Conduct: organized execution and respect for clients, properties, and project teams.
  • Long-Term Durability: work built to handle weather, use, and time.
