What We Offer

Our Exterior Services

EIFS Stucco Installation

Complete Exterior Insulation Finishing Systems installed with proper insulation boards, mesh reinforcement, base coat, primer, and finish coat. Our EIFS systems improve energy efficiency, durability, and provide a clean modern exterior appearance.

Moulding Design & Installation

Custom exterior mouldings installed around windows, doors, columns, and architectural features. We focus on sharp edges, symmetry, and precision detailing to enhance curb appeal and elevate the overall appearance of your home.

Exterior Painting

Professional exterior painting services for stucco surfaces. We prepare surfaces properly, repair minor imperfections, and apply durable coatings that protect against moisture, UV exposure, and seasonal temperature changes.

Veneer Stone Installation

Decorative veneer stone installation for exterior walls, entrances, columns, and feature areas. Our work adds texture, strength, and timeless architectural character to both residential and commercial properties.

ACM Panel & Cladding

Professional aluminum composite material panel installation for modern residential and commercial exteriors. Clean lines, sleek finishes, and durable cladding systems built to withstand harsh Canadian weather.

Roofline, Soffit, Fascia & Siding

High-quality exterior systems including eavestroughs, downspouts, soffit, fascia, leaf guards, and siding to protect the home from moisture while improving ventilation and appearance.

Caulking

Expert caulking for windows, doors, joints, and exterior connections to prevent water penetration, reduce drafts, and improve long-term protection.

Stucco Repair

Repair of cracks, impact damage, water-related deterioration, and aging stucco surfaces. We carefully blend textures and colours to restore structural integrity without visible patchwork.

Parging

Foundation parging that protects exposed concrete from moisture penetration while improving overall appearance with smooth, durable finishes designed for freeze-thaw conditions.
